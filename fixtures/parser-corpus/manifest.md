## Generation and privacy decisions

The owner authorized computer operation of installed FL Studio to create disposable synthetic projects and a clean 2024 comparison pair. F01, F04, and F05 were saved through the installed FL Studio 2024 GUI from its Empty template. F04 and F05 were originally saved sequentially with the base tempo changed from 140 to 141 BPM. The corrected pair is equal length and differs only at the two tempo-event bytes; F05 was rebuilt from the corrected F04 using the original F05 tempo value. F06 and F11 were produced by the owner solely for parser fixtures in their matching FL Studio builds. Those five genuine saves use a built-in Sampler with File `(none)`; a channel is not a sample reference. F12 is a separate synthetic sample-reference case described below. No external plugin or personal production project was imported.

The owner approved public use of the synthetic project content on 2026-09-27 and then requested correction after privacy review found embedded application registration metadata in F01/F04/F05/F06 and non-Public local paths in F06/F11. F01/F04/F05/F06 are derived from those genuine saves by removing the registration event; F06 and F11 replace their local paths with same-length Public Documents fixture paths. The owner renewed public inclusion approval for the exact nine corrected fixture bytes on 2026-09-27 after privacy review. An earlier unsanitized commit remains publicly accessible; the owner accepted that exposure for this merge, with removal left as a separate follow-up. The owner had separately attested that F06 and F11 are synthetic and may be included publicly, and explicitly authorized assigning the 2026 project to reserved slot F11. The owner also approved the exact F07-F10 byte operations and expected typed outcomes, including public inclusion after matching hashes and privacy checks, on 2026-09-27. The owner explicitly recorded F02/F03 as `not covered` because no runnable FL Studio 20/21 build exists here. The license/provenance for the project content is owner-approved synthetic content with no third-party material or external attribution requirement.

They are deliberately modified byte streams, not FL Studio saves. Offsets are zero-based. The original `FLdt` length is 111293 at offsets 18-21 (`bd b2 01 00`); F01 has one channel at offsets 10-11 (`01 00`). The first event ID is `c7` at offset 22, its length byte `0c` at offset 23, its 12-byte payload spans offsets 24-35, and the next event begins at offset 36.
