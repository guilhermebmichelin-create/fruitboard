## Generation and privacy decisions

The owner authorized computer operation of installed FL Studio to create disposable synthetic projects and a clean 2024 comparison pair. F01, F04, and F05 were saved through the installed FL Studio 2024 GUI from its Empty template. F04 and F05 were originally saved sequentially with the base tempo changed from 140 to 141 BPM. The corrected pair is equal length and differs only at the two tempo-event bytes; F05 was rebuilt from the corrected F04 using the original F05 tempo value. F06 and F11 were produced by the owner solely for parser fixtures in their matching FL Studio builds. No audio, external plugin, or personal project was imported. The built-in Sampler channel has `File (none)`; a channel is not a sample reference.

The owner approved public use of the synthetic project content on 2026-09-27 and then requested correction after privacy review found embedded application registration metadata in F01/F04/F05/F06 and non-Public local paths in F06/F11. F01/F04/F05/F06 are derived from those genuine saves by removing the registration event; F06 and F11 replace their local paths with same-length Public Documents fixture paths. The corrected bytes need renewed privacy review and matching-build validation. The owner had separately attested that F06 and F11 are synthetic and may be included publicly, and explicitly authorized assigning the 2026 project to reserved slot F11. The owner also approved the exact F07-F10 byte operations and expected typed outcomes, including public inclusion after matching hashes and privacy checks, on 2026-09-27. The owner explicitly recorded F02/F03 as `not covered` because no runnable FL Studio 20/21 build exists here. The license/provenance for the project content is owner-approved synthetic content with no third-party material or external attribution requirement.

The original saves were inspected in their matching builds before correction. The corrected F01/F04/F05/F06 bytes have passed bounded event-stream and binary privacy scans; the corrected F11 passed the binary path scan, but its 2026 event stream includes a newer opaque event that limits this static parser's coverage. All five need matching-build validation. F07-F10 were regenerated independently from the corrected F01 after checking its exact bytes and edited offsets; malformed derivatives were never opened in FL Studio. The corrected corpus has no embedded registration event in the FL 2024/2025 saves or non-Public Windows user-home path in the scanned byte alignments. This scan does not replace PR privacy review. App screenshots remain private outside Git because app chrome can display account information. Each fixture is independently copied and SHA-256 checked; no hardlink is used.

## Approved byte-derived robustness fixtures

All four derive **independently** from F01 SHA-256 `d3caba4e6e1a2cd47074b7b6e0f115d9d896e6da2e95705baff0777ef06b30ae` (111315 bytes, FL Studio saved build 24.1.0.4225). They are deliberately modified byte streams, not FL Studio saves. Offsets are zero-based. The original `FLdt` length is 111293 at offsets 18-21 (`bd b2 01 00`); F01 has one channel at offsets 10-11 (`01 00`). The first event ID is `c7` at offset 22, its length byte `0c` at offset 23, its 12-byte payload spans offsets 24-35, and the next event begins at offset 36. These operations and their expected typed outcomes were approved before creation on 2026-09-27. No parser result was used to set these expectations.
